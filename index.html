About me

I’m a cybersecurity academic and applied security practitioner with a PhD in Computer Science, specialising in AI-driven malware and ransomware detection and classification. My MSc in Advanced Control and Systems Engineering focused on physical layer security in wireless sensor networks.

I’m a Teaching and Research Assistant in Cybersecurity at the University of Bradford, where I teach Network Security and Business Systems Security to MSc students and Database Management to undergraduates. I also work as a cybersecurity specialist with Nabafat.AI in Sacramento, California.

From 2012 to 2025 I worked at the Nigerian Atomic Energy Commission, securing infrastructure against adversary attacks and supporting manpower training and capacity development. That experience shapes how I approach research: detection methods have to work where systems are remote, constrained and critical.

My work connects research-grade AI systems, enterprise detection engineering, industrial and operational technology security, and research-led teaching. I bring theoretical depth and practical operational capability to complex, distributed and regulated environments.
